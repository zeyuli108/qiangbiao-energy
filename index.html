Core Products 核心产品

Primary 主产品

Tire-derived oil (industrial heavy oil / fuel oil) 轮胎油（工业重油 / 燃料油）

Obtained via high-temperature pyrolysis of scrap tires. Mainly used as fuel for industrial boilers and furnaces; may also be evaluated as a feedstock for chemical applications. In some lower-spec engine scenarios (e.g., certain agricultural machinery or marine uses), further processing may make it suitable—this must be confirmed by testing against equipment requirements. It should not be treated as a drop-in substitute for commercial diesel. Calorific value and combustion performance should be based on third-party lab results and site conditions. We describe the product as a high-calorific industrial fuel oil, with details provided for buyer comparison. 由废轮胎高温裂解获得，主要用作工业锅炉、熔炉等场景的燃料；也可作为化工原料方向进行评估。部分对燃料品质要求不高的场景（如农用机械、船舶等），经进一步处理后可能适用，需按设备要求实测验证，不宜直接等同于商品柴油。热值与燃烧表现以第三方化验及现场工况为准；我们提供高热值工业燃料油方向的产品说明，便于采购方比选。

Secondary 辅产品

Carbon black 炭黑

Suitable for rubber reinforcement and for industrial uses such as inks, coatings, and plastics. Specifications follow batch documentation. 可用于橡胶补强，以及油墨、涂料、塑料等工业材料方向，具体指标以批次资料为准。

Secondary 辅产品

Recovered steel wire 废钢丝

Reusable for wire mesh, wire brushes, springs, welding electrode cores, and related metalworking applications. 可回用于钢丝网、钢丝刷、弹簧、焊条芯材等金属加工场景。
