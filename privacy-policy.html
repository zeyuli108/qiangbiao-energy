1. Scope

This Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share personal information when you:

2. Information we collect

Depending on the form or channel, we may collect:

  • Identity and contact details: name, phone number, email address, company name;
  • Inquiry details: product interest (e.g. fuel oil, carbon black, steel wire), approximate volume, buyer/agent role, and free-text messages you choose to send;

We do not intentionally collect special-category data (e.g. health, biometrics) through these forms.

  • to respond to your commercial inquiry (quotation, sampling, product specs, partnership discussion);
  • to verify contact details and follow up by phone, email, or WhatsApp;
  • to improve our ads, forms, and customer service (including measuring lead quality);
  • to comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use Instant Form data for unrelated marketing without a lawful basis / your further consent where required.

4. Legal basis

Where required, we rely on: your consent (submitting the form / contacting us); performance of pre-contractual steps at your request; and/or our legitimate interests in operating a local industrial business and responding to B2B inquiries, balanced against your rights.

5. Sharing

  • service providers who help us operate email, CRM, or IT systems, under confidentiality;

Staff or authorised partners handling Tanzania-based sales may access inquiries solely to reply to you.

6. International transfers

Because we use Meta and cloud tools, your information may be processed on servers outside Tanzania. We take reasonable steps consistent with applicable law and platform contracts to protect it.

7. Retention

Lead and inquiry records are kept only as long as needed for sales follow-up, dispute handling, and legal requirements—typically up to 24 months after the last meaningful contact, unless a longer period is required (e.g. ongoing contract or legal claim). You may ask us to delete earlier where the law allows.

8. Security

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or withdrawal of consent for processing based on consent. Contact us at the email/phone above. You may also lodge a complaint with Tanzania’s Personal Data Protection Commission where applicable.
